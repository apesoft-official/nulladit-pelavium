# VESTIGAVIUM

**Constitutional Delivery Accountability Protocol**  
Apesoft Canada · NULLADIT · PELAVIUM

**Pronunciation:** ves-tih-GAY-vee-um  
**IPA:** /vɛs.tɪˈɡeɪ.vi.əm/

**Origin:** A coined, Latin-inspired proper noun derived from *vestigium*, meaning footprint, trace, track or evidence left behind.

## Definition

**Vestigavium (noun):** A system of cryptographically verifiable accountability through which an action leaves an enduring, attributable trace, while preserving the privacy and independence of those who did not commit it.

## Philosophical intention

Responsibility follows conduct, not association. No participant should inherit responsibility merely because their communication passed through independently operated infrastructure. Evidence must not turn accountability into surveillance; suspicion must be distinguished from proven misconduct.

## Constitutional purpose

VESTIGAVIUM is proposed to establish cryptographic attribution of network activity, delivery receipts and PELAVIUM security observations while preserving the confidentiality of communicating parties. Accountability should be directed toward the correct actor.

Proposed requirements include signed node attribution for delivery and receipt records; tamper-evident commitments to lawfully obtained, proportionate security observations; encrypted off-chain evidence with access and retention controls; and no public disclosure of sender/receiver relationships or raw identity metadata. A node signature establishes an attestation, not necessarily the truth of its contents. Independent verification is required where applicable.

Using a node does not by itself establish consent to or participation in its independent misconduct. This principle does not confer legal immunity, override applicable law, or excuse a participant's own conduct.

## Motto

> Every action leaves a trace. Responsibility follows its author.

**Foundational philosophy:** Accountability without complicity. Evidence without surveillance. Privacy without impunity.
