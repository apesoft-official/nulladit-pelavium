# Public Accessibility and Private Administration

NULLADIT shall distribute public documentation, source releases and installers without mandatory identity-provider login. Hosting providers are replaceable infrastructure, not authorities over participation.

Public peer endpoints shall be narrowly scoped, protocol-authenticated where appropriate, rate limited and independently tested. Public health responses shall disclose only minimal service status. Administrative dashboards, secrets, local account data and management endpoints remain private. Never remove hostname-wide protection before establishing and testing the narrower boundary. Cloudflare is optional; independent operators may use compatible transport alternatives.

Windows releases must publish build provenance and SHA-256 checksums, verify artifacts and pursue trusted Authenticode signing and timestamping. Never instruct customers to disable endpoint protection. Clearly distinguish unsigned preview builds from production releases.

A reachable tunnel is not proof of a decentralized network. Production claims require independent node enrollment, authenticated discovery, encrypted delivery and restart recovery tests. Keep mining paused until its separate governance and proof gates are satisfied. Public accessibility does not override DIGITAL-INVIOLABILITY.md or authorize surveillance.

This is an engineering policy, not evidence that these controls are deployed or that any release is certified.
