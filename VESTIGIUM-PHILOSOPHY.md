# VESTIGIUM — The Doctrine of Evidentiary Restraint

**Apesoft Canada · NULLADIT · Introduced November 27, 2026**

**Evidence without surveillance. Prove the violation, not the conversation.**

## Coining the technical term

From Latin *vestigium*, a footprint or trace. Apesoft Canada adopts VESTIGIUM as a technical term for a deliberately restricted cryptographic record of an attributable network event, designed to establish integrity, responsibility or demonstrable misconduct without unnecessarily exposing protected communications, relationships or identities. This is a proposed technical meaning, not a claim to have invented the Latin word or registered a trademark.

## Presumption of Digital Inviolability

Participation in NULLADIT shall not constitute consent to surveillance. Apesoft Canada has no inherent contractual entitlement to access, decrypt, reconstruct, inspect or demand disclosure of protected communications, private relationships, cryptographic secrets or vulnerable personal information.

An exceptional claim for narrowly restricted access may arise only where all four conditions are met:

1. A demonstrable violation of PELAVIUM's security or integrity requirements has occurred.
2. VESTIGIUM has recorded independently verifiable cryptographic evidence attributable to the responsible participant.
3. That evidence has undergone independent review and cannot reasonably be explained by ordinary network activity, technical failure or innocent participation.
4. The proposed access is necessary, proportionate and independently lawful.

Suspicion alone shall never constitute authorization. Nor shall a violation extinguish a participant's general right to privacy. The burden remains with the party seeking access. These conditions create no decryption capability, universal key, backdoor or surveillance entitlement.

## Responsibility follows conduct, not association

A delivery or storage receipt should establish only the relevant protocol operation and accountable participant, with replay protection and independent verification. A recipient does not automatically assume responsibility for a node's actions, and a node does not automatically assume responsibility for message contents. Cryptographic evidence of a defined event is not by itself evidence of intent, guilt or legal liability.

PELAVIUM protects private activity; VESTIGIUM seeks to establish attributable violations of network integrity. Neither mandate should unnecessarily defeat the other.

## Accountability earned through service

The proposed eligibility model for privileged evidentiary functions depends on verified, rewarded Proof-of-Service, not wealth or mere account creation. Eligibility is not authorization; independent review, purpose limitation and all four conditions remain necessary. This is a design objective, not an implemented or deployed privilege.

## Evidence minimization

Collect only what is necessary; separate public commitments from confidential evidence; minimize linkability, retention and access; guard against replay, collusion and false attribution. Never publish protected communications, raw evidence, identities, relationship graphs or secrets on-chain. A commitment alone cannot prove misconduct. Test against timing correlation, malicious evidence, compromised nodes and innocent failure; publish limitations and negative findings.

No documentation, proof-of-service reward, governance vote or contract commitment can itself authorize surveillance or create a decryption capability.
