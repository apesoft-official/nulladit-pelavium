## Architecture and legal position
NSELCBA separates operator terms and service-exchange attestations from custody, token issuance and settlement. An attestation is not independent proof of performance, payment or fair market value. Canadian barter recognition does not create a tax or regulatory exemption. Relevant authorities include Income Tax Act s.9, Excise Tax Act ss.153 and 188.2, PCMLTFA s.5, BC Electronic Transactions Act and Canada Evidence Act ss.31.1–31.3. Independent legal review remains outstanding.

NULLADIT preserves confidential communications while pursuing accountable service verification. NODSEL means NULLADIT Deployed Open Source Encryption Logic; PELAVIUM remains experimental research. Vestigium is intended to support accountability without disclosing conversations. The proposed nine-seat constitutional panel is not enacted by NSELCBA and must not be confused with the ten-seat adjudication registry. Mining activation is not authorized by this update.
