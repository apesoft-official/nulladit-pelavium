# Reciprocal Observation Theory (ROT)

**RESEARCH ONLY — NOT DEPLOYED.**

## Founding proposition
Privacy should not depend solely upon the inability of an observer to decipher information, but also upon the limitations of what observation can establish.

## The Observer Paradox
PELAVIUM draws conceptual inspiration from quantum measurement and observer-dependent knowledge. Classical software does not inherit quantum mechanical guarantees. The underlying digital state exists independently of whether an observer knows it.

## Research principles
- Observer-dependent knowledge: an accessible representation need not describe the complete system.
- Measurement disturbance: active interactions may change system state and produce evidence of interaction.
- Reciprocal observation: legitimate, proportionate accountability and resource costs may accompany active requests.
- Informational uncertainty: observable activity does not necessarily establish its human significance.

## Constitutional limits
Passive observation may be undetectable. Plaintext disclosure and endpoint compromise may reveal genuine communications. Do not claim universal observer detection, attribution or anonymity. No retaliatory exposure, unauthorized collection or publication of personal information. Preserve legitimate users' privacy and minimize audit data.

## Relationship to PELAVIUM
ROT complements Snow Blindness and Bidirectional Stochastic Cover Activity (BSCA). It is a research hypothesis concerning observation, knowledge and accountability, not a demonstrated cryptographic property. Evaluate claims under explicit threat models, publish negative results, and require independent review. The existing engineering constitution remains binding. No production deployment or promotion to NODSEL is authorized.
