# Constitution of Bidirectional Stochastic Cover Activity

RESEARCH ONLY — NOT DEPLOYED.

## Article I — Purpose
Investigate whether observable envelope submissions and discoveries disclose information about genuine human communication beyond prior knowledge. Both genuine and synthetic activity are within the research scope.

## Article II — Scientific integrity
Statistical indistinguishability is a hypothesis, not an established guarantee. Define the observer, assumptions, sample size, observation duration and baseline before interpreting results. Publish negative results and uncertainty. Failure to reject a null hypothesis is not proof of privacy.

## Article III — Longitudinal evaluation
Assess submission and discovery observations individually and jointly, including repeated observations over time. Distinguish event-level classification from relationship inference. Account for class imbalance and multiple comparisons.

## Article IV — Resource responsibility
Measure bandwidth, storage, latency and device resource costs. A running client performs activity; a wallet alone cannot. Offline participation and endpoint compromise remain limitations.

## Article V — Research boundary
Use synthetic data and isolated experiments. Do not publish production secrets or real user traffic. No research result authorizes deployment, mining changes or promotion to NODSEL. Independent review and separate deployment approval remain mandatory.

## Article VI — Evidence
Record reproducible methods and distinguish executed tests from proposed tests. No claim of statistical impossibility may be made without a formal proof under explicit assumptions.
